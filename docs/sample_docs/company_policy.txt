COMPANY POLICY: CODE OF CONDUCT
Last Updated: April 2026

1. GENERAL EXPECTATIONS
All employees are expected to maintain a professional environment. This 
includes being on time for meetings, respecting deadlines, and treating 
colleagues with kindness and professionalism.

2. REMOTE WORK & TOOLS
- Use company Slack and Email for all work-related talk.
- Keep your calendar up to date so the team knows your availability.
- Ensure your internet connection is stable for video calls.

3. DATA SECURITY
- Never share your login passwords with anyone.
- Only use company-approved software for project tasks.
- If you notice any suspicious activity, report it to IT immediately.

4. DIVERSITY & INCLUSION
We believe in equal opportunity. We do not tolerate any form of 
harassment or discrimination. Everyone should feel safe and 
welcome at work.

5. VIOLATIONS
Breaking these rules may lead to disciplinary action, including 
termination of employment.
